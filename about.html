About IndustHub

The industrial testing platform of Shenzhen TB-TEK Technologies Limited. (Turbotai), focused on quick connection technology, leak testing solutions and industrial test equipment for manufacturers worldwide.

Founded in Shenzhen in 2006, focused on connection and testing

Shenzhen TB-TEK Technologies Limited. (Turbotai)in 2006Founded in Shenzhen, China, and a long-standing provider of reliable, efficient connection and testing solutions for manufacturers worldwide, specialising in quick connection technology, leak testing solutions and industrial test equipment.
