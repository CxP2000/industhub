Applications

Typical applications for liquid cooling quick couplings

Liquid cooling quick couplings cover data centres, power devices and production line testing where reliable non-spill connections matter.

Data centre server liquid cooling

In-rack cold plate / piping quick connects; blind-mate series suit confined-space docking.

Cold plate and power device cooling

Cooling loop connections for cold plates in power modules and inverters; flat-face non-spill reduces maintenance risk.

CDU coolant distribution units

High-flow connections inside and outside the coolant distribution unit; C783 / C78 suit high flow.

End-of-line leak testing

End-to-end air-tight / liquid loop testing before shipment; fast connect and disconnect improve takt time.
