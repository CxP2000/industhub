E Series Electrical Test Connectors

The E Series is a key part of the C9 docking plate assembly, providing long-life, fast electrical connections between the tray C9M docking module and the customer interface. It covers low-voltage signals, drive current and high-current probes, with custom wiring on request. Socket and plug in the same circuit must be paired.

All parameters and model numbers below are taken from the E Series electrical test connector handbook. Pin diameter, pin count, cable type, cable length and interface form are subject to final selection confirmation.

E10T Signal Connector

Manual · signal · 100,000 mating cycles

E10T is a signal connector with a broad, mostly custom-interface product range. With the right probe and structure, mating life can exceed 100,000 cycles. Once the interface is fixed, customers usually specify connector form (straight / 90 degree elbow), shielding, high-voltage withstand, cable type and cable length, so actual model numbers vary considerably and need detailed confirmation with an engineer.

E10D / E10DZ High-Voltage Circular Pin / Socket

High voltage · pin diameter 8–14 mm · gold/silver plating

E10D is used for high-current circular pin interfaces, with long-life socket contacts on the E10D side; E10DZ is used for circular socket interfaces, with long-life pin contacts on the E10DZ side. High-life test solutions are available for pin diameters of 8 / 10 / 12 / 14 mm, with typical mating life above 100,000 cycles. Compared with standard automotive connectors, it uses highly reliable gold / silver plated socket contacts.

E10JP / E10J Tab Electrode / Clip Female

Medium current 40–80A · high current 150–400A

E10JP is used for tab-type high-current interfaces, with clip pins or tab electrodes on the E10JP side; 40–80A medium current uses clip probe structures, while 150–400A high current uses the GripSeal self-adjusting clip structure for longer life. E10J is used for clip-type female interfaces, with tab electrodes on the E10J side made from high-strength beryllium copper with surface plating for wear resistance and longer life.

E10U UVW Busbar Connector

3 / 6 pin · twist-lock handle · high current

E10U is a busbar interface electrical test connector for mainstream busbars on the market, usually in 3-pin or 6-pin configurations, with other forms such as Type 2 available. A twist-lock handle connection (no screws) is reliable and easy to operate, commonly used in high-current applications.

E10C High-Current Male-Female Terminal

Withstand voltage / dielectric strength testing

E10 terminal-type quick connectors and E10C high-current male-female terminals are used in electrical testing such as insulation and dielectric withstand tests, offering long mating life, maintainability, one-second connection with instant testing and low running cost.

Custom Enquiries

Confirm customisation by interface requirements

E Series electrical test connectors can be customised. Confirm the following with an engineer before ordering:

  • 3D drawings of the electrical interface
  • Interfaces to be connected and their quantity
  • Technical specification options (cable type, shielding, withstand voltage, HVIL, etc.)
  • Cable length and other special requirements
  • Final 3D drawings / physical samples provided after order
  • Confirm whether prototyping is needed first
Send your port drawings for a pairing recommendation

Need to confirm a specific E Series selection or custom design?

Send 3D drawings of the electrical interface, the number of connections, technical specification options and cable length, and we will match the model and pin/socket configuration.
