C Series Male-Female Quick Couplings

The C Series covers automatic and manual male-female quick couplings in six series, C20 / C21 / C22 / C23 / C26 / C27, with working pressures from 50 bar to 550 bar and bores of 3–38 mm. All-stainless or aluminium construction, non-spill design, supporting tray-based automated docking and liquid cooling / hydraulic manual plug-in. Socket and plug in the same circuit must be paired.

Selection Support

Confirm pairing by circuit requirements

Confirm these three points to lock in the matching C Series socket and plug models.

Confirm the port form

Provide the end connection form, thread or tube specification for both socket and plug, plus existing piping drawings or photos.

Confirm media and parameters

State the media type, working pressure and required bore, and whether blind mate (automated docking) or a high-flow structure is needed.

Confirm actuation

The C Series covers automatic docking and manual plug-in: choose C20 / C26 / C27 for automated lines and C21 / C22 / C23 for on-site manual use; lock the model by pressure 50–550 bar and bore 3–38 mm.
