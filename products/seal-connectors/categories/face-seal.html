Selection Guide

Information needed to select a face seal connector

Provide face diameter, surface condition, valve port structure, pressure, media and actuation.

1. Face diameter

Flat face diameter and available sealing area determine the face seal structure.

2. Surface condition

Flatness, scratches or residue affect sealing performance.

3. Pressure and media

Pressure and media compatibility determine the seal material.

4. Actuation

Manual, fixture or automated operation determines the actuation structure.
