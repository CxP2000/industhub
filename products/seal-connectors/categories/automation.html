Recommended combination

Why G10 + G15 + G10D + G15T

An automated leak test station usually needs: sealing the tube OD (G10), sealing the tube ID (G15), plus G10D (tube OD) / G15T (tube ID) pneumatic-float seals when a small OD, high flow or automatic centring is required. All four are pneumatically actuated to form a ready-to-integrate quick sealing and leak test combination.

Station workflow

Typical automated station

A standard cycle from loading to data acquisition.

1. Load and locate

The part enters the station, is clamped pneumatically and its ports align with the sealing heads.

2. G10 seals the tube OD

Seals the outer tube wall quickly, forming one side of the test chamber.

3. G15 seals the tube ID

Expands inside the bore to form the other side of the test chamber.

4. Charge and leak test

Charge the test media, capture the pressure curve and decide pass or fail.
