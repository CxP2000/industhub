G10 Tube OD Quick Seal Connector

Pneumatic external-expansion tube OD quick seal connector, vacuum to 35 bar, aluminium or stainless steel body, for automated leak testing, functional testing and repeated connections on tubing.

Model overview

Pneumatic expansion seals tightly against the tube outside wall

G10 admits air through a rear pilot port to drive a piston that compresses the internal seal outward against the tube wall. Suited to repeated line connections, leak testing and functional testing.

Product features

Core design of the G10 Series

Pneumatic expansion sealing

Air enters through the rear pilot port; the piston compresses the seal outward against the tube wall for fast one-handed or fixture-based connection.

Built for high-volume automation

Designed for high-output automated production testing: improves takt time, reduces wasted failed tests and increases test stability.

Straight-through test port

A straight flow path reduces pressure and flow loss, maintaining high flow for leak and functional testing.

Replaceable seals, multiple materials

Main seal Neoprene / silicone and inner seal Buna-N can be replaced individually, lowering maintenance cost.
