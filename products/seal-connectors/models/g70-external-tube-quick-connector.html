G70 External-Clamp Quick Seal Connector

External clamp seal: jaws grip the bead outside diameter of the tube end from outside and form a face seal. Independent of tube ID, suited to leak, run-in and ageing tests on irregular tubes such as cooling water and fuel lines.

Model overview

External clamp, face seal

G70 uses a sliding sleeve to drive split jaws that grip the bead outside diameter, with a face seal forming a gas-tight connection; the straight flow path reduces pressure loss.

At a Glance

Typical uses

Consider this external-clamp solution when the workpiece is a beaded or irregular tube end that should not be sealed from the internal bore.

Product features

Core design of the G70 Series

External clamp sealing

Jaws grip the bead outside diameter from outside and form a gas-tight face seal, independent of tube ID, suited to irregular and beaded tubes.

Slide sleeve operation · straight-through design

The sliding sleeve opens and closes the jaws; the straight flow path reduces pressure and flow loss for high-flow leak and functional testing.

Safety confirmation ring

The in-position ring gives visual / tactile feedback so pressure is only applied after clamping, improving operating safety.

Damage-free series (G70P family)

G70P / G70SP / G70RP / G70NP use a polymer jacket for external clamping, avoiding scratches on aluminium and engineering plastic surfaces, vacuum to 10 Bar.

Applications

Typical applications for G70

Real applications in the catalog cover leak, run-in and ageing tests on irregular tubes and cooling / fuel lines, plus new energy vehicles and refrigeration.

EV battery cold plate testing

Sealing connections for battery cold plates and cooling circuits; the G70P damage-free series suits easily scratched surfaces.

Motor cooling and engine water channels

Leak and run-in testing of motor cooling systems and engine cooling water channels.

Engine assembly / grooved tubes

Engine assembly testing and grooved ports such as hot/cold water pipes, drive unit inlets and compressor inlets/outlets.

Refrigeration flared tubes / fuel lines

G70K for refrigeration flared tubes (50 Bar); the fuel line version meets J2044.
