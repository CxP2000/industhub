Product features

Where this series adds value

Protects internal threads

Fast clamping reduces repeated screwing and protects internal thread surfaces from wear.

Multiple structural versions

Button, lever, extended-neck and pneumatic versions can be assessed for different port depths and operating space.

High-pressure capability

Once thread, seal and fixture conditions are confirmed, this series can be evaluated for high-pressure test tasks.

A production-ready sealing method

A repeatable push-to-seal process reduces operator variation and false readings caused by inconsistent threaded connections.

Operation logic

Press to connect, instead of repeated screwing

1. Align with the internal thread

Align the connector with the internal-thread port and confirm the lead-in chamfer and depth are suitable.

2. Press or actuate

Press the button, lever or pneumatic actuator so the seal enters and locks onto the threaded port.

3. Test and release

After the pressure or leak test, release the actuator, remove the connector cleanly and start the next cycle.

Selection Support

Confirm the port before choosing a model

Internal-thread port details are critical: thread depth, chamfer, wall thickness and available space all determine the right G80 structure.

01

Thread assessment

Confirm thread standard, pitch, effective depth, and whether the entry has a chamfer or counterbore.

02

Pressure and media check

Pressure, gaseous or liquid media and temperature determine the seal material and body structure.

03

Operating space

Button, lever, extended-neck or pneumatic actuation should match the real station layout.

04

Confirm against drawings

Drawings or port photos help avoid a connector that seals but cannot be operated in the fixture.
