G20A Low-Pressure External-Clamp Connector

For low pressure · damage-free external clamp · lever operation · wide sealing range. For test ports below 3 Bar, the seal expands radially around the tube OD; only rubber contacts the sealing face so the part's finish is not damaged, and a rear nylon lever flips down to clamp.

Low-pressure external clamp · damage-free sealing

One press of the lever seals the tube OD

G20A is designed for test pressures below 3 Bar: the seal expands radially to form a large sealing area on the tube OD. The rear lever flips 95° to clamp, and a self-adjusting spring adapts to different tube diameters and extends seal life.

Product features

Core design of G20A

Radial OD sealing

The seal expands radially to form a large sealing area on the tube OD for stable, reliable sealing.

Self-adjusting spring

Automatically adjusts seal compression to suit different tube diameters, extending seal life and fitting many tube types.

No finish damage

Only the rubber seal contacts the sealing face; no hard parts touch it, protecting the tube finish.

Tool-free maintenance

Disassembles without tools for simpler ongoing maintenance.

Lever operation

The moulded lever is light and wear-resistant, reducing operating force during long repeated use.

Wide sealing range

18 models cover Ø 0.8 – 23.0 mm, from small to large bore tubing.
