G85 High-Pressure External Thread Connector

For external-thread ports in plumbing appliances, water heaters, instruments and high-pressure test stations: threaded jaws lock with a face seal, completing air-tightness and pressure test connections without repeated screwing.

Fast sealing connection for external-thread ports without repeated screwing, improving connection efficiency and reducing thread wear from frequent testing.

Product features

Core design of G85

Modular actuation

Pneumatic, button valve and lever modes cover different benches, fixtures and line requirements.

Threaded jaw locking

Threaded jaws carry the mechanical load in high-pressure testing, avoiding thread damage while improving connection efficiency.

316 stainless steel body

Excellent mechanical properties, strong and wear-resistant, ensuring ultra-high pressure ratings and significantly longer connector life.

High flow and face seal

A large-bore flow path greatly reduces flow loss and impact on run-in tests; the face seal is easy to replace with low maintenance cost, and seal materials can be chosen for different fluids.

Up to 350 Bar

For high-pressure testing of pressure sensors, hydraulic valves and similar; the plumbing series is rated 20 Bar, so choose the series by station pressure.

Operation logic

Three-step connection

1. Press the handle down

For lever versions, press the handle down first to open the threaded jaws.

2. Push into the threaded port

Insert the test fitting until the thread face fully contacts the main face seal.

3. Release to complete clamping and sealing

Release the handle; the jaws retract to grip the external thread and complete the sealed connection.
