G72K Irregular Tube Port Quick Connector

Designed for test ports with side threaded holes: lever-driven quick thread lock, a small rotation compresses the sealing head, max. 50 Bar; the replaceable POM sealing head does not damage high-precision test ports.

Quick connector for side-thread ports

Quick thread lock · damage-free seal

G72K targets test ports with side threaded holes: quick thread locking without screwing; flip the lever to engage and lock automatically, compress the sealing head for a high-pressure seal, then release to remove after testing.

Product features

Core design of G72K

Lever-driven locking

Flip the lever to open the jaws onto the thread, then a slight clockwise turn compresses the sealing head for a leak-free high-pressure test.

Side-thread port connection

Designed for test ports with side threaded holes; quick thread locking, no screwing, stable and reliable connection.

Aluminium body

The lightweight aluminium body is reliable and low-effort, suited to repeated test stations.

Damage-free sealing head

The sealing head is made of replaceable POM and does not damage high-precision test ports.

Quick seal change

Seals are easy to replace, with simple maintenance and longer life.

Customisable ports

The body can be customised with flexible port configuration; the test port can be specified as needed.
