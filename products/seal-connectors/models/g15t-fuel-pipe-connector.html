G15T Tube ID Pneumatic-Float Quick Seal Connector

Float compensation variant of the G15 family with pneumatic expansion sealing; the side drive/test port works with a GFL float compensation module for automatic centring, damage-free connection and quick seal changes, designed for automated leak and functional testing.

Model overview

Pneumatic expansion + float compensation, automatic centring

G15T uses drive air to move the housing forward and compress the front seal into an expansion seal; the side drive/test port works with a GFL float compensation module to automatically offset workpiece alignment errors.

Product features

Core design of the G15T Series

Pneumatic float · expansion seal

Drive air moves the housing forward to compress the front seal into an expansion seal; the side drive/test port makes it easy to pair with a float compensation module.

Float compensation, automatic centring

With a GFL float compensation module it automatically offsets workpiece alignment error and solves test alignment problems.

Damage-free connection

The port contacting the test piece is wrapped in rubber/PEEK to protect the workpiece surface from damage.

Quick seal change

The centre rod unscrews quickly, making seal replacement simpler than a circlip design, with shorter maintenance time and longer life.

Applications

Typical applications for G15T

The G15T Series is a tube ID, pneumatic-float sealing solution for automated leak and functional testing that needs automatic centring, damage-free connection and quick seal changes.

Water channel and radiator testing

Leak and functional testing of tube bores in motor water channels, radiators and similar.

Turbocharger / fuel pump

Automated repeated leak testing of oil and gas line ports.

Plastic ports / irregular parts

Damage-free connection protects surfaces, suited to plastic and irregular ports.

Automatic centring stations

With a GFL float compensation module it accommodates alignment offset and improves consistency.
