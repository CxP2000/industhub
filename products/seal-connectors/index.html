How to select

Confirm these first

Send these details to our application engineers for a faster series match.

Port type

First establish whether the port is threaded, tube OD, tube ID, face or irregular — this sets the basic sealing structure.

Pressure and media

Working pressure range and media (air / water glycol / fuel) determine the seal and body materials.

Seal material

NBR / FKM / EPDM / Neoprene selected by media and temperature.

Actuation

Manual, pneumatic or automated fixture integration affects cycle time and consistency.

Temperature range

Confirm the working temperature range so it stays within the seal's limits.

Tolerances and dimensions

Provide port dimensions and drawings to match the clamping and sealing method precisely.

Not sure which series fits?

Send the port type, pressure, media, actuation and drawings or photos, and we will match a suitable model.
