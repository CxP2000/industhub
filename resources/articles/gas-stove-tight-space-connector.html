Quick connection in the tight working space of a gas stove

With only 60–65 mm of length and 50 mm of width available, a standard connector will not fit; a custom G60N-085M straight G1/2 external-thread connector solves fast connection of the gas port.

News Company News Case Studies FAQ

Case name

Case

A well-known domestic gas stove / kitchen appliance manufacturer purchased quick connectors for a newly developed model, used for run-in, air-tightness and pressure testing. The original test port used a hand-tightened nut seal, which was slow, and the available operating space was extremely tight: only 60–65 mm long and 50 mm wide.

Home appliancesAir-tightness testingCustom solutions

Customer requirements

Challenge

Space constraint

Operating space is only 60–65 mm long and 50 mm wide; a standard connector cannot fit.

Structural requirement

The connector must be straight-through, no longer than 50 mm and no more than 35 mm in outside diameter.

Port requirement

The rear fluid port uses an 11.4 mm radiused barb for easy gas hose connection.

Solution

Solution

Based on the thread dimensions provided, the standard G60-085 external-thread connector would have matched, but at 80 mm long and 40 mm in diameter it clearly could not fit this space. After several rounds of discussion and continuous optimisation between the sales and engineering teams and the customer, a custom barb-ended G60N-085M connector was developed — currently the smallest OD G1/2 external-thread connector, built for the demanding space of the gas stove industry.

This custom connector is used for fast connection of the gas port in gas stove air-tightness and run-in testing; the rear radiused barb connects directly to the gas hose, completing the test connection without extra adaptors.

Test feedback

Feedback

After fitting the custom G60N-085M connector, both run-in and leak testing of the gas stove went smoothly. The compact connector makes fast connection easy even in tight space; the rear radiused barb mates with the gas hose, ensuring accurate and efficient testing. The customer was satisfied with the cooperation and expressed interest in a long-term relationship.
