Leak-Test Sealing for Glass, PVC and Metal Hoses

The challenge with dissimilar-material ports is not clamping force but matching seal material and thermal expansion coefficient.

News Company News Case Studies FAQ

Why glass ports are harder to seal than metal ones

Challenge

Common leak test connectors are stainless steel or aluminium cylinders for routine sealing of metal tubes and chambers, mature in performance and practicality. But sealing elements for glass, PVC tube and metal hose are considerably harder.

Leak test connectors usually need both firmness and air-tightness. Glass is fragile and weak under pressure, so glass-to-metal sealing is far more demanding than conventional sealing. Standard connectors can withstand very high temperature and pressure and can be fitted with higher-spec seals for high pressure, high mechanical stress and high thermal stress.

Matched seal vs compression seal: two mechanisms

Mechanism

Leak test connectors can be further designed by application and operation, and various terminations of non-hermetic connectors can be reused. The main difference between a hermetic connector with a glass-to-metal seal and a standard connector is the sealing material: one is a matched seal, requiring the thermal expansion coefficients of metal and glass to match; the other is a compression seal, where the housing metal's thermal expansion coefficient is far greater than that of the glass and contacts, so the glass is under compressive stress as the parts cool from sintering temperature.

Advantages of glass-to-metal seal connectors

Advantages

These connectors are used where moisture, atmospheric pollutants or other contaminants must be effectively kept out of the connected device, covering military and high-reliability commercial electronics, optoelectronic, electromechanical and hydraulic devices, electrical connections in vacuum high-voltage equipment, and devices filled with inert gas or gas whose composition must remain unchanged.

In high temperature, high pressure or corrosive fluid environments, seals and other materials must be specified to suit actual conditions. Hermetic connectors are stronger than standard ones, with a compact sealed section that is easy to make slender for reaching into extremely limited spaces; because they are small and hermetic, a cavity can be placed behind the connector to house filters or other electronics and sealed together.

Common seal material types

Materials

Glass-to-metal seal types include compression seals, Kovar matched seals, Inconel seals, and special seals made from non-magnetic thermocouples and rare materials. Each seal material suits different applications and must meet the end user's specific needs.

Hermetic sections face higher sealing standards and different usage scenarios, so repeated testing and validation against the actual application field is needed to reach a reliable sealing test solution.
