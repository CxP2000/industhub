Integrated docking for tray-based testing

Docking System

Battery, motor and controller systems have many electrical performance test items across final assembly lines, quality control and powertrain scenarios. If testing still relies on traditional threaded connections and manual tightening, it easily becomes a line bottleneck. A gas-electric-liquid docking system for tray-based line testing has a body comprising a mounting plate, modular electrical connectors and automatic male-female couplings, covering integrated fast docking of gas, liquid and electrical circuits, suited to frequent mating and stable contact.

Electrical port and housing air-tight sealing

Electrical & Housing

Matching electrical test connectors are mainly manual, with automated and robot-operated versions also available, covering signal lines, high current, busbars, tab contacts and other interface types, significantly reducing integrator design time.

Air-tightness testing of motor, controller and battery pack housings needs fast, reliable sealing of high-voltage ports, explosion-proof valve ports, waterproof vent threaded holes and other varied ports: G25F seals the inner walls of electrical interfaces for battery PACK and motor controller housings; the G80 Series plugs and connects internal-thread holes including waterproof vent threads and high-pressure test ports.

Cooling ports and water-flow testing

Cooling Loop

For beaded tube ends, J2044 ports and similar in cooling systems, G70 and G70P damage-free quick seal connectors can be used. G70P uses polymer jaws that avoid scratching the surface while keeping a reliable seal; face sealing with sliding-sleeve operation reduces operator effort. The G90 Series meets fast sealing needs for VDA standard ports, with straight-through, L-type, mini compact and damage-free locking structures; it seals on the internal O-ring of the fuel port female so no extra seal consumables are needed, staying stable and easy to maintain at high frequency. Where internal expansion sealing is needed, the G35 Series has its own locking structure for greater test safety.

In water-flow and ageing tests, coolant or glycol mixtures demand tight flow control. The G70 Series is optimised for flow demand, limiting flow impact while keeping sealing performance; C Series male-female couplings were developed to solve fluid spillage with non-spill connections, especially important where liquids and electronics share the test environment, and their stainless steel construction meets long-term durability under demanding conditions.
