Leak testing copper tubes in water heater tanks

For water heater tanks with coiled copper tubes and variable end-port positions, G72 Series seal connectors for beaded / irregular tube ends provide a fast air-tight test connection.

News Company News Case Studies FAQ

Case name

Case

Domestic water heater, kitchen appliance and hot water system manufacturers need to leak test tank welds during water heater assembly. Tank water lines are coiled copper tubes, tank fittings differ between products and the final port position is hard to fix, so a quickly adaptable test connector is needed.

Home appliancesRefrigerationAir-tightness testing

Customer requirements

Challenge

Port characteristics

Tank water lines are coiled copper tube; the end port position varies by product and tank fittings are not uniform across models.

Test task

Air-tightness testing of tank welds during assembly needs fast, reliable port plugging and repeated connection.

On-site constraints

Operator workload must be reduced and dedicated fixtures for every tank model avoided where possible.

Solution

Solution

Based on the customer's test needs, the application engineer recommended the GripSeal G72 Series seal connectors for beaded / irregular tube ends: a lightweight all-aluminium body that puts little force on the copper tube; button or pneumatic actuation to reduce operator effort; a compact structure sealing on the copper tube end, with low demands on port position, so no dedicated fixture is needed — saving cost.

G72 SeriesAluminium bodyButton / pneumatic actuationBeaded tube · irregular portNo dedicated fixture

Solution benefits

Advantages

Quick sealing connections

One-second connection and instant sealing shorten connection time at the assembly test station.

Reliable sealing

Suits the customer's pressure test environment with stable repeated connection.

Light and easy to operate

The lightweight all-aluminium body and button or pneumatic actuation reduce operator effort.

No dedicated fixture

Low demands on copper tube end port position, fits multiple models and saves fixture cost.

Customer feedback

Feedback

After using GripSeal quick connectors for water heater tank copper tube testing, the customer reported clearly lower operator effort, a light yet sturdy and safer build, lower risk of false test readings, one-second connection and instant sealing, and that the product and service met expectations.
